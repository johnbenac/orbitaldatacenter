Orbital Data Center LinkedIn banner — Blender orbital visualization

Spacecraft geometry and original materials: exported from Orbital Data Center v21,
using its live-text mission starter. This is an illustrative design configuration.
The rendered scene is not evidence that a mission concept is feasible.

Earth day and cloud textures: Solar System Scope / INOVE.
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: mapped to 3D Earth and cloud spheres; day-map saturation and brightness
adjusted; rendered with sunlight and an atmospheric scattering shell.

Starfield skybox: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020.
Visualization by Ernie Wright.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/
Source: starmap_2020_4k.exr, rotated and exposure-adjusted in the scene.

Lettering: Inter, bundled with its license in ../src/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt.
Project logo and banner layout: existing Orbital Data Center and LinkedIn banner work.

Reproduce the orbital scene with Blender:
blender --background --python render-orbit.py
Use -- --preview for the smaller draft render.
The .blend file packs all image textures and can be opened independently.
